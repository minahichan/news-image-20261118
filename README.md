An infographic summing up the world's news of Wednesday, 18 November 2026:
the US–Iran escalation in the Gulf, Ukraine's Kerch Bridge strike, the Gaza
winter warning, COP31's decisive phase, the Romania earthquake, and more.

## Stories in the image

- **US–Iran war escalates** — US says it sank five Iranian oil tankers in
  the Gulf of Oman; Iran hit back at ships and US sites in Bahrain and Jordan.
- **Ukraine** — Kerch Bridge hit a second night; Trump–Putin–Xi summit Thursday.
- **Gaza** — UN warns of cold deaths as 1.7m displaced face winter unsheltered.
- **Climate** — COP31 in Antalya enters its decisive phase (144 disputed items).
- **Romania** — 5.3-magnitude quake, no major damage reported.
- **Also today** — Quad leaders meet in Manila; Russian military plane crash
  kills 19 in Siberia; North Korea fires missiles after a US–South Korea drill.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024) is drawn programmatically with Pillow — every word is
set in DejaVu Sans, so the text stays crisp and legible.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` into the output directory, so both runs write
byte-identical files.
